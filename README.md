# Financial & ESG Reporting Dashboard - Coconut Oil Ltd

## Overview
12-month CSRD-ready reporting workbook for Coconut Oil Ltd (Nov 2025 - Oct 2026).

## Key Results
- Total Cost: £63,940
- Total CO2e: 146.2 tonnes (146,174 kg)
- Electricity: 265,760 kWh
- Water: 8,218 m3
- Sites: Rotterdam Plant, Marseille Depot, Dublin Head Office

## What's Inside
- **Raw-Data:** Monthly data by site
- **KPI_Summary:** Totals, averages, KPIs
- **Dashboard:** Charts - CO2e by Site & CO2e by Month
- **Methodology:** Scope 1, 2 & 3 emission factors & audit trail

## Methodology
- Scope 1 Diesel: Litres x 2.68 kg CO2e / litre
- Scope 2 Electricity: kWh x 0.212 kg CO2e / kWh
- Scope 3 Landfill Waste: kg x 0.58 kg CO2e / kg
